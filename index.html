The person behind the recipes

Meet Betty.

Betty's story and recipes are rooted in making the most of what you have. Growing up during the Second World War, she learned practical cooking skills alongside her mother — knowledge that lived on in the dishes collected in this book.

This website is a way to celebrate her cookbook and share its recipes with a new generation.
